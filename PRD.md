# Touristo - Product Requirement Document (PRD)

## 1. Introduction
Touristo is envisioned as a mobile-first marketplace that connects verified tour companies with travelers across Pakistan. It operates as an asset-light platform, similar to Airbnb or Booking.com, but specifically for domestic tourism. The primary goal is to simplify the process of discovering, booking, and experiencing local tours.

## 2. Vision Statement
To become the leading digital platform for domestic tourism in Pakistan, fostering trust and convenience between verified tour operators and travelers.

## 3. Goals & Objectives
*   **Connect:** Facilitate seamless connections between verified tour companies and interested travelers.
*   **Discover:** Provide a rich discovery experience for diverse tour packages and destinations.
*   **Book:** Offer a secure and straightforward booking and payment system.
*   **Experience:** Enhance the travel experience with features like live weather updates, trip planning, and communication tools.
*   **Verify:** Ensure quality and trust through a verification system for tour companies and a review system for users.

## 4. Target Audience
*   **Primary:** Individual travelers and groups seeking domestic tours in Pakistan.
*   **Secondary:** Verified tour companies looking to expand their reach and manage bookings online.

## 5. Core Features
### 5.1 User Management
*   Sign-up/Login for travelers.
*   Profile creation and management.

### 5.2 Tour Company Management
*   Dedicated registration flow for tour companies.
*   Company profile creation and dashboard for managing packages and bookings.

### 5.3 Discovery & Search
*   Home/Discover screen showcasing featured packages.
*   Search functionality to find packages by destination, date, etc.
*   Destination exploration pages with details.
*   Host/Company discovery tab.

### 5.4 Package & Booking
*   Detailed package information pages.
*   Booking and integrated payment processing.
*   My Trips section to manage upcoming and past bookings.

### 5.5 Communication & Planning
*   AI-powered Trip Planner chatbot for personalized suggestions.
*   Host/Company profile pages for more information.
*   Post-trip reviews and ratings.

### 5.6 Travel Experience Enhancements
*   Live weather and road status information for active trips.
*   Live location sharing during trips.
*   In-app Emergency SOS feature.

## 6. Non-Goals
*   Directly operating or providing tours (asset-light model).
*   Supporting international tourism initially.
*   Complex multi-city itinerary planning (initially).

## 7. Success Metrics
*   Number of registered verified tour companies.
*   Number of successful bookings made.
*   User engagement metrics (session duration, screen views).
*   User satisfaction scores from reviews.
*   Growth in monthly active users.
